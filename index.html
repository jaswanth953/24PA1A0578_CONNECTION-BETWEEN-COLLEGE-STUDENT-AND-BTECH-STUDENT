Frequently Asked Questions

Which college is best for CSE?

It depends on your rank and location. While IITs and NITs are top-tier, many private universities offer great placements for CSE. Use our "Ask Seniors" feature to get specific advice!

How to choose a branch?

Evaluate your interests—if you like coding, go for CSE/IT; if you like hardware, ECE/Mechanical might be better. Talk to our mentors to understand what you'll study in each branch.

Is CampusConnect free for students?

Yes, connecting with seniors and reading college reviews is completely free for all Intermediate students.
